Manual override: [...] is strong and <...> weak; adjacent annotations force syllable breaks. For example, [Ve]<ry> preserves capitalization while forcing two syllables. Escape literal delimiters with a backslash.

Notation guide
[strong]
One explicit strong/stressed syllable.
<weak>
One explicit weak/unstressed syllable.
[Ve]<ry>
Adjacent annotations force syllable boundaries without changing capitalization or inserting spaces.
\[ \] \< \>
Escaped delimiters are literal text, not notation.
Manual annotation always wins. Unannotated text is analyzed automatically and original spelling/capitalization is retained.
